# NBA v1.3.4 — participation exposure sensitivity

November 19's earlier Discord card went 2/3 and its later card 0/3. Portland -7.5 and Minnesota +6.5 won; the later under 235.5 and Minnesota +1.5 lost. GS +8.5 lost in both. No record is erased, and this review does not conclude that fresher lineups should be ignored because the earlier card won more bets.

## Model review

Larger injury multipliers, higher injury caps, faster/slower team recency, weaker/stronger prior rating shrinkage and lower prior retention were screened with chronological 2025–26 features. Changes that looked better in an earlier period did not improve consistently in the later period. None was promoted. Historical absence proxies use actual box-score participants, which is optimistic information versus a morning projected lineup. These are research screens, not betting-profit tests.

A concrete mismatch remains: team offense/defense ratings weight games by recency and possessions, but the injury baseline's participation fraction uses an unweighted season average. Recent prolonged absences or recent returns can therefore have a different exposure in the strength baseline than in the injury adjustment.

The data builder now stores independent game-level participation summaries. A helper computes the exact recency/possession-weighted share, strictly before the prediction date. Missing player logs return an unavailable value rather than falsely identifying all players as absent. Legacy inputs retain their prior behavior. No market data is read by this path.

Replacing the central exposure with this weighted share slightly improved the full 2025–26 analytic winner log loss (0.59106 to 0.59071), but slightly worsened the 226-game current-season screen (0.60333 to 0.60356). Consequently the central forecast is retained. V1.3.4 instead freezes an additional 50,000-draw sensitivity distribution when the alternate exposure materially changes scoring targets. A recommended contract must survive this distribution as well as all existing roster, sampling-error, calibration, fee, price and score-alignment checks. This is an input-model robustness check, not a confidence interval or demonstrated ROI gain. It can remove winning bets as well as losing bets.

The new selection-policy identity is `participation-roster-quote-stress-v4`. Discord explains the exposure check when present. The stage-one central score simulation and RNG stream are unchanged; all four November 19 archived unconditional margin and total histograms reproduced exactly after adding the alternative. Additional participation logs for that replay were reconstructed retrospectively from strictly prior game dates, and are not claimed to be original immutable source snapshots. Exposure scenarios average over other uncertain players; every interaction with every conditional roster state is not separately simulated.

## Validation

All offline NBA CI checks pass, including source firewall, fresh-input checks, signed Discord routing, joint-roster stress, execution/quote guardrails, score alignment, daily learning and delivery. New tests verify recency/possession weights, no same-day leakage, missing-log handling, fragile-edge rejection and the actual production simulation worker preserving central histograms while recording an alternate distribution. No Discord posts were made by local validation.

`research/participation-screen-nov20.json` records the analytic before/after screen; `research/participation-screen.mjs` reproduces it. Analytic probabilities are normal approximations from scoring targets and prior-season residual SD, not full possession-level simulations.
