# Prediction and recommendation review — November 7, 2026

The November 6 verified Discord record was 1–6 on primary bets despite 12/13 correct outright winners. Winner accuracy, exact-score accuracy, recommendation hit rate and profitability are separate metrics. This patch retains model probability strictly above 50%, positive fee-adjusted EV, pregame timing and existing execution checks. An 80% realized win rate is not promised.

## Recommendation policy

Recommend only when positive EV and probability above 50% survive sensitivity checks; among eligible lines, prefer the highest conservative probability, then EV. The sensitivity minimum is a stress diagnostic, not a calibrated individual confidence interval. Frozen point forecasts and historical records are not rewritten. Market prices remain outside the forecasting model.

## Permanent evaluation gap corrected

The previous daily learner evaluated outright winners, not the probabilities of the primary contracts actually sent to Discord. `scripts/bet_learning.py` captures only after successful publication, preserves NO BET supersessions, rejects post-start capture, verifies archived hashes, and reconciles results using the actual handicap/total contract. The latest pregame recommendation per event counts once, even across model/market changes. Game-order contracts cannot be graded from aggregate LoL series scores and remain ungraded. Pushes/voids are excluded from binary win/loss statistics.

Daily learning now imports these durable artifacts and reports probability reliability, Brier/log loss, flat-cost return assumptions and chronological calibration proposals separately for each model, policy and market family. At least 500 settled recommendations over 30 days, with separate validation/test periods, are required by the existing proposal evaluator; nothing is automatically promoted. Actual fills are unknown; the ledger measures recommendations, not user transactions.

Validation includes outcome settlement when the predicted game winner differs from the recommended spread, KC +1.5 losing despite an exact T1 3–1 forecast, duplicate-run deduplication, later NO BET supersession, late publication rejection and archive tampering. No live Discord test messages were sent.

## NBA evidence

An exploratory CDF-temperature calibration was fitted on the existing 1,231-game 2025–26 chronological replay and evaluated on 90 later games (October 20–November 1, 2026). Results use the stored 4,000-simulation histograms. Absence inputs use hindsight box-score proxies and are optimistic relative to morning availability. Fixed spread/total diagnostic lines are correlated and are not historical bets or executable quotes.

Winner beta 1.129 worsened held-out log loss from .623675 to .627440. Spread beta .9644 slightly improved .472279 to .470677, but the date-cluster bootstrap improvement interval [-.000264, .003315] crossed zero. Total beta .9856 likewise failed to establish improvement. None is adopted.

For risk screening only, a prior-season date-cluster bootstrap of average overconfidence on model-favoured fixed lines gave upper 97.5th-percentile values .000565 for winners, .010098 for spreads and .007974 for totals. Round up to .001/.011/.008 probability-point reserves, subtracted only in recommendation screening in addition to Monte Carlo error. These are not individual forecast error bounds or evidence of betting profit. Existing 3–8-point edge limits remain. Rank qualifying lines by the conservative probability rather than maximum edge.
