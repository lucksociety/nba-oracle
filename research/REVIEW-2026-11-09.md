## NBA: sensitivity to uncertain availability

The previous selector evaluated only the availability-weighted mixture. A bet can have mixture probability above 50% and positive EV while failing if a questionable player is ruled in or out. Capture the original Bernoulli availability decisions from the existing game simulations without drawing additional random numbers. Freeze per-player IN and OUT margin/total histograms with the base prediction, before any prices are fetched. Each conditional retains other players' original availability mixtures, and IN includes the existing restricted-minutes uncertainty.

Both venue adapters now require probability above 50% and fee-inclusive positive EV in every recorded individual-player conditional after Monte Carlo error and the existing prior-season reserve. Fewer than 1,000 draws for any conditional means no recommendation until a rerun/resolved status. This is not a check of every joint roster combination or a calibrated individual confidence interval. No-effect players already excluded by the engine are not introduced as new injury penalties. No uncertain modeled players means no additional restriction. Old forecasts with uncertain availability and missing conditional data fail closed.

`replay-availability.mjs` verifies archived input/prediction SHA-256 values and recreates November 8 from the original sanitized pregame inputs, date and seeds. Exact unconditional total and margin histograms match all seven original games. `availability-replay-2026-11-08.json` shows all five original recommendations still qualify. This is explicitly not a retrospective win-rate improvement. In particular, losing MIL +12.5 and MIA–SAC Over 238.5 still pass; the patch does not explain away those misses.

The selection policy is versioned separately in the delivered-bet learner so future reliability reports can evaluate the new rule without pooling prior recommendations. Forecast version and scoring weights stay unchanged. The prior market firewall, score agreement, fresh injury inputs, liquidity, pregame and EV guards remain active.

Validation: seeded capture-on/off simulations match every score; conditional histogram partitions reconstruct the whole distribution; actual worker-thread output matches direct simulation; fragile conditional probability/EV, insufficient draws and old missing data are rejected. Offline model, routing, learning and execution regressions pass. No test posts to Discord.
