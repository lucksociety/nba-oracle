# NBA November 11 review and safeguards

November 10's final delivered card went 0–2: Miami–Utah Over 224.5 lost at 224, Orlando–Sacramento Over 219.5 lost at 207. The earlier Miami Over 222.5 won. Four of six displayed winner forecasts were correct; winner accuracy is not total-contract accuracy.

## Findings and changes

Miami's uncertain-player OUT sensitivity lower probability was 0.601614, barely above the published 0.597052 cost. One cent of adverse quote movement erased that margin. A new execution-price stress requires positive EV after both roster/calibration stress and a one-cent higher ask. Jupiter uses a conservative rounded whole-contract fee allowance (ceil-cent of 0.07*p*(1-p)); actual order/fractional-fill fees depend on quantity. Polymarket stress uses its own market fee schedule, including zero where explicitly provided. The live quote, not the stressed price, remains the displayed execution price.

Orlando's 0.618113 probability versus 0.587157 original cost passed the prior minimum edge by only 0.000956. Conservative fee allowance raises cost to 0.59, making its edge 0.028113, below the unchanged three-point minimum. It still passes the one-cent stress in isolation. These selection changes do not explain or correct its 19-point total forecast error.

The prior availability checks evaluated each uncertain player separately while averaging over all other players. That can hide failure when multiple players are OUT together. Freeze joint roster-state histograms from the existing Bernoulli draws, before market access. Enumerate at most eight uncertain players, checking joint states with at least 1% modeled mixture mass, in addition to every existing individual conditional. Each state needs 1,000 draws and must satisfy the same >50%, calibration reserve, sampling-error and positive stressed-EV requirements. Missing/unsupported joint forecasts fail closed for multiple uncertain players. States below 1% mass and explicit joint minutes-limit states are not covered. This is a robustness screen under the model's independent availability assumptions, not proof of real-world calibration.

The same simulated scores, RNG stream and unconditional probabilities are retained. No scoring, pace or player-impact coefficient was changed. A blanket downward total adjustment is unsupported by this slate: actual minus predicted displayed totals averaged only -0.83 points across the six games, with large errors in both directions.

Polymarket fallback liquidity now counts depth only at the exact quoted ask and observes its published minimum order size; contracts a cent higher cannot justify cheaper EV. Daily delivered-bet reports now separate over/under and other market directions, retaining model/selection-policy isolation and the existing chronological sample-size gates. New selection policy: joint-roster-quote-stress-v3. The existing daily learning job runs this report; small samples do not automatically change probabilities.

No later injuries, scores or prices are passed to the engine. This is a retrospective diagnostic, not untouched holdout evidence.

Original primary cards: Nov 8 3/5, Nov 9 5/6, Nov 10 0/2 (8/13 total). Combined retained 3/5 versus original 8/13. The replay also removes winners. There is NO demonstrated win-rate improvement on this small sample; alternate selections were not reconstructed because complete historical books are unavailable. Do not report a 100% retrospective record from the two rejected November 10 losses.

Validation: all offline model, joint-state partition/RNG, quote stress, venue fees, exact-depth, freshness, firewall, Discord routing/delivery, and Python learning/evergreen tests pass. New tests verify a joint failure concealed by passing marginal checks, missing and oversized joint forecasts, bounded enumeration, material-state cutoff, and preservation of fee-free Polymarket pricing.
